title: "Simulated sensors"
description: "Try a demo measurement without connecting hardware."

Demo experiments offer simulated sensors that replay a fixed example measurement.
They are available only in experiments owned by a Demo organization. Ordinary
experiments keep their physical device connections.

## On the web

1. Open a demo experiment's **Design** tab.
2. Choose **Simulated MultispeQ**, **Simulated Ambit**, or **Simulated MiniPAR** in
   the connection selector. Match the sensor to the demo workbook's protocol.
3. Connect and run the workbook. No Bluetooth or USB permission is needed.
4. Review the result and its **Simulated** label. Outputs from later macro and
   question cells retain that label.

A simulator accepts its matching demo protocol. A different protocol reports an
error rather than returning an unrelated example. Repeating a measurement
returns the same readings. Each run receives its own workbook output.

Changing experiment disconnects the simulated sensor. Read-only demo workbooks
can run locally; running them does not edit the published workbook.

## On Android

Choose a demo experiment and start its measurement flow, then open **Devices** and select **Simulated MultispeQ**.
Continue through the normal measurement and result steps. Android supports
MultispeQ simulation only.

The result and its entry in **Recent Measurements** show **Simulated**, including
when an upload is pending or has failed. The marker stays with the saved payload
and any later upload. Switching to an ordinary experiment removes the simulated
connection and hides its reconnect shortcut. Physical devices remain available
for reconnecting.

The upload error in this recording comes from the local
test environment, where AWS uploads are unavailable; the measurement remains saved

## What the readings mean

The current recordings are **synthetic placeholders**, awaiting hardware-team
recordings. They are shaped for each sensor's protocol but are not observations
from a physical sample. Their firmware version is recorded as `synthetic`, and
their protocol version as `placeholder-v1`. Do not use these values as research
measurements or as evidence of instrument performance.

In experiment data tables, **Simulated** (`is_simulated`) marks simulated readings
and **Guest** (`is_guest`) marks readings uploaded through the guest path. These
are independent boolean columns: a signed-in user's simulated reading is not a
guest reading. In the filter picker, choose **Simulated** or **Guest**, then **is**
or **is not** and **Yes** or **No**. Exports retain both boolean columns. Historical
readings without these markers default to false.
