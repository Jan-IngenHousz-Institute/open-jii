description: "Request JII instruments through openJII Equipment and record collection, custody and returns."
---

openJII Equipment is where researchers borrow and return the Jan IngenHousz
Institute's field instruments, and see which ones are sending data to openJII.
Use openJII to manage experiments and photosynthesis data.

## Who can borrow

Loans are for JII staff and invited partner researchers. The equipment register
is invitation-only. Signing in does not reserve an instrument.

Open [Equipment](https://equipment.openjii.org) and choose an available sign-in
method. When offered, **Continue with openJII** uses your openJII session. A new
account using that method is preapproved if it belongs to the JII organization.
Other researchers can request access with their name, organization and reason.
An administrator reviews that request. After approval, sign in again and accept
the current terms when asked.

If you already use GitHub for Equipment, keep using that method. Matching email
addresses do not link accounts. Read Equipment's
[user guide](https://equipment.openjii.org/docs)
for the current options, or [contact the service](https://equipment.openjii.org/contact)
for account or loan questions.

## Request instruments

For a field deployment, open **Proposals** and choose **New proposal**. Describe
the experiment, sites, collection and return dates, and measurements you need.
Review the suggested kit, explain changes and submit the proposal. Follow its
status in Proposals and respond if the team asks for changes. Approved tracked
items become equipment requests.

For a direct request, open **Requests** and choose **Request instruments**. Enter
the instrument type, quantity, destination and start/end dates. Check the dates
in UTC before saving. Follow the request in the board or table.

A request needs approval before checkout. Approval plans capacity; it does not
guarantee a particular serial number or record a handover. The team selects
individual units for collection. Requesting instruments does not guarantee
availability.

## Collect, use and return

1. At collection, scan the instrument's QR label or find its asset tag in
   Equipment. Sign in if prompted. Opening the record does not check out the unit.
2. Agree the holder, location and expected return date with the person recording
   the handover. Check that any attached kit units match the physical kit.
3. Verify the holder and timeline after checkout. A partially delivered request
   stays **Approved**. **Fulfilled** means the requested quantity has been checked
   out; the instruments still need to be returned.
4. At the end of the loan, record the return, location and condition with the
   team. Check that active custody has ended. A damaged return needs a description
   and leaves the unit in maintenance.

Use **Report error** on the unit's record to describe a fault. Reporting a fault
and recording a return are separate actions. Contact the team about changes or
extensions; a proposal extension request does not change custody dates until
approved. Canceling remaining demand does not return units already checked out.

See Equipment's [borrowing overview](https://equipment.openjii.org/how-it-works)
and [user guide](https://equipment.openjii.org/docs)
for the current workflow. The register is shared with approved members, so keep
sensitive research data and private personal details out of equipment notes.
