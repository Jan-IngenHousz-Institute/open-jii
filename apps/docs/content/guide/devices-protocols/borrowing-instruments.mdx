title: Borrowing instruments
description: Find and request Jan IngenHousz Institute instruments in openJII Equipment.

openJII Equipment is where researchers borrow and return the Jan IngenHousz
Institute's field instruments, and see which ones are sending data to openJII.
Borrowing is limited to JII and partner researchers with approved access.
An openJII account alone does not grant access to the equipment register.

<Callout type="info">
  Equipment is being prepared for launch. The links below use its planned public address and become
  available after the domain is connected. Ask your JII contact about access and availability in the
  meantime.
</Callout>

## Request an instrument

1. Open [openJII Equipment](https://equipment.openjii.org) and sign in using an
   enabled method for your approved account.
2. Browse the equipment register to find the instrument type you need.
3. Open Requests and submit the type, quantity and dates for your work.
4. Wait for approval and arrange collection with the equipment manager. A request
   does not check an instrument out; the manager records the allocated units.

The asset page shows the unit's holder, location, planned return and history.
Arrange a return with the manager so they can record it and check the condition.
Measurement data and experiments remain in [openJII](https://openjii.org).

## Open a device's equipment record

On a JII-owned device's Overview tab in openJII, JII organization members see
**See in openJII Equipment** in the About card. It opens Equipment in a new tab
and resolves the device's identifier to its registered asset. Sign in to Equipment
if prompted; openJII membership does not replace Equipment's own admission check.

If Equipment cannot find one matching asset, ask an equipment administrator to
check the registration. The link does not create an asset or copy measurement
data. Devices owned by another organization do not show this link.
