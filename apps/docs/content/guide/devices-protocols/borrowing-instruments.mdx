openJII Equipment is where researchers borrow and return the Jan IngenHousz
Institute's field instruments, and see which ones are sending data to openJII.
Use openJII to manage experiments and photosynthesis data.

## Who can borrow

Loans are for JII staff and invited partner researchers. The equipment register
is invitation-only. Signing in does not reserve an instrument.

Open [Equipment](https://equipment.openjii.org) and choose an available sign-in
method. When offered, **Continue with openJII** uses your openJII session. A new
account using that method is preapproved if it belongs to the JII organization.
Other researchers can request access with their name, organization and reason.
An administrator reviews that request. After approval, sign in again and accept
the current terms when asked.

If you already use GitHub for Equipment, keep using that method. Matching email
addresses do not link accounts. For account or loan questions,
[contact the service](https://equipment.openjii.org/contact).

## Request, collect and return instruments

Equipment's own guides describe the borrowing workflow and stay current with it:

- The [borrowing overview](https://equipment.openjii.org/how-it-works) explains
  how a loan works, from request to return.
- The [user guide](https://equipment.openjii.org/docs), available once you are
  signed in, walks through proposing a deployment, making a direct request,
  collecting instruments by their QR label, and recording a return or a fault.

The register is shared with approved members, so keep sensitive research data and
private personal details out of equipment notes.
