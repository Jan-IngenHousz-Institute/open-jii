---
title: "Installing the desktop app"
description: "Use openJII as an app on macOS, Windows or Linux."
---

The openJII desktop app shows the same platform as the web, in a window of its own, and you sign
in with an email code. It adds what a browser tab can't: platform notifications reach your
computer's notification area, and measurement devices connect over USB cable or Bluetooth whatever
browser the computer has.

<Callout type="info">
  In a browser, Chrome and Edge connect devices over USB and Bluetooth, Firefox over USB only, and
  Safari not at all. In the desktop app they connect the same way whatever browser you use.
</Callout>

## Download

| System | Download | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| macOS | [Apple silicon](https://download.openjii.org/desktop/latest/openJII-arm64.dmg) or [Intel](https://download.openjii.org/desktop/latest/openJII-x64.dmg) | macOS 13 or newer |
| Windows | [Installer](https://download.openjii.org/desktop/latest/openJII-Setup.exe) | Windows 10 or newer |
| Linux | [AppImage](https://download.openjii.org/desktop/latest/openJII.AppImage) or [deb package](https://download.openjii.org/desktop/latest/openJII.deb) | A 64-bit Intel or AMD processor |

1. Download the file for your system.
2. Open it and follow the installer. On macOS, drag openJII into Applications.
3. Open openJII. It checks for updates by itself from then on.

## Sign in with an email code

Enter your email address and the code we send you. The app signs in with an email code only, so
GitHub, ORCID and passkeys are not offered there. Signing out of the app does not sign you out of
your browser.

## Connect a device

Choose **Connect** on a protocol or a workbook as you would on the web. The app lists the devices
it can reach, and you pick one.

- **USB cable:** plug the device in. It appears in the list straight away.
- **Bluetooth:** pair the device in your computer's Bluetooth settings first, then choose
  **USB/serial** and pick it from the list. A MultispeQ asks for the pairing code `1234`.
- **macOS:** the first time a measurement uses Bluetooth, macOS asks whether openJII may use it.
  Choose **Allow**. You can change this later under System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then
  Bluetooth.
- **Linux:** if a USB device does not appear, your account may need to be in the `dialout` group.
  Run `sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER`, then sign out of the computer and back in.

## If the app can't reach openJII

The app shows a page that says so, with **Try again**. Measurements already saved on the platform
are not affected. Check the computer's internet connection, then try again.
