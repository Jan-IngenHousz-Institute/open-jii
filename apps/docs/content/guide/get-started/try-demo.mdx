---
title: "Try the demo"
description: "Explore public experiments without an account or a physical sensor."
---

These steps apply once **Try the demo** is enabled on the sign-in screen. If the
button is absent, demo mode is switched off. While you are offline or availability
has not been confirmed, the button stays visible and disabled with a connection
message. Connect to the internet to start a guest session.

## Enter on the web

1. Open the sign-in page and choose **Try the demo**, below the passkey button.
2. Optionally enter a nickname. Leave it blank to use Guest. Do not enter an email
   or web address. Names can contain up to 60 characters.
3. Read the notice and terms, then choose **Continue to the demo**.

The demo organization opens with its experiments. A banner shows your nickname,
the seven-day guest lifetime and **Leave demo**. You can read public content and
run measurements in demo experiments. Guests cannot create experiments, protocols,
macros, workbooks or organizations, request a project transfer, or request
membership in other experiments or organizations. They cannot upload data files,
annotate rows or export data on the web, fork workbooks, or see collaborator
and member lists. The platform hides all of these from guests, along with
Devices, Calibrations, notifications and account settings.

Measurements you take in the demo stay public permanently. Use synthetic sample
names and do not enter personal or private research information. If you allow
location access on Android, your GPS coordinates are included in the measurement.
Your chosen name appears publicly beside your measurements while the guest account
exists. After the seven-day account cleanup, they show **Former guest**. Do not use
your real name unless you want it shown. The name resolves from the current account;
it is not copied into the permanent measurement rows.

## Enter on Android

On the sign-in screen, choose **Try the demo** below GitHub and ORCID. The same
nickname prompt opens. After continuing, Data shows the demo experiments. The app
caches their pinned workbooks for offline use after the first successful load.
You can browse public experiments through Discover. Guests do not see its join-code
entry, or join and membership-request actions on ordinary experiments and organizations.
Demo experiments keep their measuring action.
If content could not load, reconnect and retry before relying on it offline.

The guest banner stays on every main tab. Recent lists only measurements taken
by your current guest session. It does not show a previous guest's measurements
or another person's cached research.

## Measure without hardware

Open the MultispeQ demo experiment and start its workbook. At the device step,
choose **Simulated MultispeQ**, connect, then run the measurement. The result is
marked **Simulated**. Android saves the marked result in Recent; open it to see
the measurement details. The web also offers simulated Ambit and MiniPAR in their
corresponding demo experiments.

These sensors replay synthetic placeholder recordings, so the numbers are for
exploration, not research. See [simulated sensors](/guide/devices-protocols/simulated-sensors)
for the recording limits.

## Leave the demo

Choose **Leave demo** in the banner and confirm. Publicly uploaded measurements
remain available, and the guest account is left for the scheduled seven-day
cleanup. Leaving does not convert it into a researcher account.

On Android, if your current guest has unsent measurements, choose:

- **Upload now** to try uploading while staying signed in. Failed or pending
  uploads keep the warning visible. Uploading requires an internet connection.
- **Leave anyway** to discard the local session and leave those unsent rows on
  the phone for guest cleanup. A later researcher or new guest cannot see or
  upload them.

Android clears local guest credentials, cached data and selections even offline
or if the server cannot confirm sign-out. Unsent guest rows expire through the
app's seven-day cleanup; unsent researcher rows are unaffected.

On the web, leaving requires confirmed sign-out. If the browser is offline or
sign-out fails, you remain in the demo with a **Try again** action. Your session
and visible data stay intact until a retry succeeds.

## Offline access and expiry

An existing Android guest can read cached demo content and save measurements
offline. Uploads and new guest sessions require a connection. Guest access ends
seven days after the original account creation, even if its session was refreshed.
At expiry, the app clears local guest access and explains how to start again.

Setting the phone clock back substantially also ends local guest access. Keep
automatic date and time enabled. Leaving or changing accounts clears prior
selections and cached query data before another identity is shown.

## Google Play reviewer path

Once demo entry is enabled, no researcher account or sensor is needed for review:
open **Try the demo**, continue with a nickname, open the MultispeQ demo in Data,
connect **Simulated MultispeQ**, run a measurement, and inspect its Simulated label
in Recent. Use **Leave demo** to finish. If a local review build cannot upload to
AWS, Recent shows the upload failure; that is not proof of a server save.
