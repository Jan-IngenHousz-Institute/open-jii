openJII saves an accepted mobile measurement to local SQLite before attempting a
cloud upload. Loss of connectivity therefore does not have to stop data
collection, provided the experiment flow was cached first.

## Before going offline

1. Sign in while online; a first login cannot be completed offline.
2. Let the app cache your experiments and their pinned workbook versions.
3. Choose each field experiment and confirm **Offline ready**.
4. Run one complete test flow with the intended device.

An experiment without an attached workbook version cannot become offline ready.
New experiments and workbook updates need a later online refresh.

![An experiment selected with the Offline ready badge](/img/mobile/offline-ready.png)

The badge applies to the selected cached flow. It does not mean a new or changed
workbook has already been refreshed.

A paused flow resumes where you left off, including offline. In the rare case
that the app no longer holds the workbook code for that flow, it shows **Can't
resume this flow** instead of its steps; choose **Exit flow** to pause or
discard it and start a new one.

The phone downloads the Python runtime from the internet. If that runtime has
not loaded before the connection drops, the result step says the macro needs an
internet connection instead of showing its output. You can still accept the
reading. The platform runs the macro once the measurement uploads, and its
output appears on the web platform. JavaScript macros run on the phone without
a connection.

## What happens to a saved reading

On **Recent**, a cloud icon at each reading shows where it stands:

| Icon                     | Meaning                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Blue cloud with an arrow | Saved on the phone and waiting to upload, or uploading now.                                                                                  |
| Green cloud with a check | Synced: the platform has the reading. A reading up to 128 KiB is confirmed by the message broker; a larger one by the platform's file store. |
| Red cloud with an alert  | The upload failed. A red line under the reading says why. The reading stays on the phone.                                                    |

The **Synced** and **Unsynced** filters split the list along the same line; a
reading waiting to upload and a failed one both count as unsynced. While uploads
run, a blue number on the upload button in the header counts them.

Uploads pause while the phone is offline. Each reading is acknowledged on its
own, including readings from a multi-device round. When an upload hits a
problem that may pass, such as a dropped connection, the app tries it again up
to three times, about 1, 4 and 15 seconds apart, before marking the reading
failed. A problem that trying again cannot fix, such as an expired session,
marks it failed at once.

A failed reading is not given up on. The app tries every unsynced reading again,
failed ones included, when it starts, when the connection returns, and when you
bring it back to the foreground.

### Why an upload failed

| Red line under the reading                      | What it means                                                         | What to do                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Could not reach the server. Swipe to try again. | The phone could not get the reading to the platform before giving up. | Wait for a connection, or swipe the reading and select **Upload**.                                                                                                                            |
| Your session expired. Sign in again to upload.  | The upload could not be authenticated as you.                         | Sign in again, then upload the reading.                                                                                                                                                       |
| You can no longer upload to this experiment.    | You no longer have access to the experiment, or it no longer exists.  | Keep the reading. If the experiment still exists, ask someone who manages it to restore your access. If it was deleted, export the reading and [get in touch](/guide/reference/getting-help). |
| The server would not accept this measurement.   | The platform refused the reading itself.                              | Keep the reading, export it as below, and [get in touch](/guide/reference/getting-help).                                                                                                      |
| Upload failed. Swipe to try again.              | Something else went wrong.                                            | Swipe the reading and select **Upload**.                                                                                                                                                      |

## Recover safely

Open **Recent** and filter to **Unsynced**. Swipe a reading and select **Upload**
to send that one; the action is hidden while the phone is offline. The upload
button in the header sends every unsynced reading after you confirm **Upload
All**; it is unavailable while uploads are already running. Do not delete a local
reading until it is synced or exported.

The mobile **Export measurements** action creates JSON, but the current
implementation selects failed and synced rows rather than every in-flight queued
row. Treat it as a recovery aid, not a substitute for confirming sync. Dataset
exports from the web platform use the formats described in
[Exporting data](/guide/data-analysis/exporting-data).
