The production mobile release is distributed for **Android** through Google Play,
published publicly as **openJII** by **OpenJII** (rated PEGI 3; the listing notes
that the app shares location).

<Callout type="info">
  There is no published iOS app. The measurement devices used today connect over USB serial or
  Bluetooth Classic, and neither channel is available to an app on iOS, so the field app ships on
  Android only.
</Callout>

1. Open the [openJII listing on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.openjii.app).
2. Install or update **openJII**.
3. Open the app while connected to the internet.
4. Sign in with email, GitHub, or ORCID.

![The openJII Android home screen, signed into the dev testing environment](/img/mobile/app-home.png)

_Captured with production app 2.16.1 (build 160). The yellow **DEV** badge
identifies the required dev environment._

## Sign in by email

Enter the email address on your openJII account. The app sends a six-digit code;
you can read that code on another device and type it into the field phone. No
password is required.

GitHub and ORCID sign-in leave the app briefly and return through the `openjii`
app link after authentication. If you close the browser before you finish, you
are back on the login screen, which says that sign-in was not completed. You can
try again or choose another way to sign in straight away.

The app does not ask for your location when you open it or sign in. It asks the
first time you start a measurement; see
[Taking measurements in the field](/guide/measuring/taking-measurements).

If you do not belong to an organization yet, Home shows a card that opens the
organization directory, which is also under Profile → Organizations. You can
browse it and ask to join from the app; see
[Find and join an organization](/guide/organizations/finding-and-joining).

## If the app can't reach openJII

When the phone cannot reach the openJII server, the login screen says
**Can't reach openJII** across the top of the form, with a **Try again** button.
The app checks the connection twice before it shows this, so one slow reply does
not trigger it. Common causes are Wi-Fi without internet access, a network that
wants you to accept terms in a browser first, and mobile data switched off.

You can keep typing your email address, or the code from your email, while the
message is showing. The sign-in buttons work again as soon as the connection
returns, a complete code is checked then, and the message goes away by itself. Tap **Try again** to check right away after you change networks.

## Prepare offline access

Your first sign-in requires connectivity. After sign-in, the app caches the
experiments you created or that were shared with you personally, and each one's
pinned workbook version. Before going offline:

- wait until the experiment shows **Offline ready**;
- connect and test the device;
- confirm that the experiment has a workbook and protocol;
- keep the app signed in.

If an experiment or workbook changes later, reconnect and open the app so it can
refresh the cache. See [Working offline & syncing](/guide/measuring/offline-sync).

<Callout type="warn">
  Do not rely on an old minimum-device specification copied from earlier docs. Check the current
  Google Play compatibility message for the actual field phone.
</Callout>
