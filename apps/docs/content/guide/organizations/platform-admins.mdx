---
title: "Platform admins"
description: "Appoint platform admins and manage the Demo organization type."
---

Platform admins manage the Demo organization type and appoint other platform admins.
This role is separate from an organization's Owner or Admin role.

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Platform admins**. Only platform
admins see this entry. The page lists the current admins and when each was appointed.
Admins supplied by deployment configuration have a **Set by deployment configuration**
label. Their role changes through deployment configuration.

To appoint someone, type part of a registered researcher's name and choose **Appoint
admin** beside the result. To remove someone appointed on this page, choose **Remove
admin** beside their name. You cannot remove your own role.

A platform admin still needs an organization's Owner permission to change its settings.
When creating an organization, or editing one they own, they can choose **Demo**. See
[Demo organizations](/guide/organizations/creating#demo-organizations) for its public
visibility rules.

Platform admins cannot sign in as someone else. API keys cannot exercise platform admin
powers, even when the key belongs to a platform admin.
