---
title: "Platform admins"
description: "Who platform admins are, how JII grants the role, and the Demo organization type they manage."
---

Platform admins are JII staff who manage the Demo organization type. The role is separate from an
organization's Owner or Admin role, and holding one gives nothing of the other.

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Platform admins**. Only platform
admins see this entry. The page lists the current admins, when each was granted the role, and
their user id.

Nobody can grant or remove the role in the app, platform admins included. JII changes it outside
the app, and a second person approves each production change. Every change is recorded with who
made it and when. To ask for the role, or to have it removed, contact JII.

A platform admin still needs an organization's Owner permission to change its settings. When
creating an organization, or editing one they own, they can choose **Demo**. See
[Demo organizations](/guide/organizations/creating#demo-organizations) for its public visibility
rules.

Platform admins cannot sign in as someone else. API keys cannot exercise platform admin powers,
even when the key belongs to a platform admin.
