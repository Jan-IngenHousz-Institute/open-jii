Everything you create in openJII is owned by exactly one workspace. By default that
is **your personal workspace** — yours alone, and nobody else can be added to it.

An **organization** is the shared alternative: a lab, a group, or a project team that
owns work collectively, so it survives people joining and leaving.

## Why make one

- **The work outlives the person.** A resource owned by your personal workspace is
  reachable only by you and whoever you shared it with individually. A resource owned
  by a lab is reachable by the lab.
- **Access stops being a checklist.** New colleagues get access by joining the
  organization, not by being added to twenty experiments one at a time.
- **Somebody is always answerable.** Every organization must keep at least one Owner,
  which is what stops work being stranded when someone leaves.

<Callout type="info">
  Your personal workspace is not an organization and never appears in the organization directory,
  your organizations list, or any invite flow. It cannot be renamed away, shared, or deleted.
</Callout>

## Where to find them

**Organizations** in the sidebar lists the ones you belong to, each with how many people
and how many resources it holds:

## The three roles

| Role       | What it means                                                                                                                                 |
| :--------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | Full control of everything the organization owns, plus the organization itself — its settings, its deletion, and making someone else an Owner |
| **Admin**  | Full control of everything the organization owns, and manages people. Cannot change the organization's own settings or delete it              |
| **Member** | Reads everything the organization owns. On experiments, Members can also contribute measurements and annotations                              |

The person who creates an organization is its first Owner.

<Callout type="info">
  Member is not a read-only role on experiments. Belonging to the lab is what makes someone a
  contributor — see [Who can access your work?](/guide/access).
</Callout>
