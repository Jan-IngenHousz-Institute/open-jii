Only an Owner can delete an organization, from its **Settings**.

## It is refused while it still owns anything

Deleting an organization does not delete its work, and does not hand that work to
anyone. So the delete is refused while the organization still owns a single experiment,
macro, protocol, workbook, device or device group. The refusal tells you how many of each
are left.

This is deliberate: published work should not disappear behind one confirmation dialog.

## Emptying it, in order

1. **Move what you want to keep.** Transfer each experiment, macro, protocol or workbook
   to another organization or to your personal workspace — see
   [Moving a resource](/guide/sharing/moving-resources).
2. **Delete what you do not.**
3. **Delete the devices and device groups.** They are the exception below.
4. **Delete the organization.**

<Callout type="warn">
  **Devices and device groups cannot be moved.** Every other resource type can be transferred out,
  but neither of these has a transfer route — so an organization holding them can only be emptied by
  *deleting* them. Deleting a device also revokes its certificate; deleting a group leaves its
  devices alone, and only undoes the grouping and whatever it was shared with. Plan for that before
  you start.
</Callout>

## What deletion takes with it

Deleting an organization removes more than the organization row:

- **Its teams go with it**, and every share those teams held is withdrawn.
- **Shares the organization itself held are withdrawn**, including shares on resources
  owned by _other_ organizations. If this lab had Can edit on a partner lab's
  experiment, that access disappears too.
- **Members are not deleted.** People keep their accounts and everything they own
  elsewhere; they simply stop being members of an organization that no longer exists.

<Callout type="info">
  Your personal workspace cannot be deleted, and does not appear anywhere in this flow. Deleting
  your **account** is a different operation with its own rules — including that it is blocked while
  you are an organization's last Owner. See [Account, security & API
  keys](/guide/get-started/account-security-api-keys).
</Callout>
