There are two ways into an organization: someone invites you, or you ask.

## Browse the directory

Go to **Organizations** and switch the filter from your own organizations to all of
them. The directory lists every **public** organization, with its name, profile and
what it chooses to show about itself — plus the private ones you already belong to, so
switching the filter never hides your own work from you.

A private organization you are **not** a member of is not listed, and there is no way to
find it. If a colleague's lab is not there, it is private and the only way in is an
invitation — ask them to send one.

## Ask to join

Open a public organization and select **Request to join**. You can add a short message
saying who you are and why, which is worth doing: an Owner or Admin sees it beside your
name when they decide.

Your request is pending until someone acts on it. You can cancel it while it is pending.

- **Approved** — you join as a **Member**: you can read everything the organization owns
  and contribute measurements and annotations to its experiments.
- **Rejected** — you are not added. Nothing else changes.

<Callout type="info">
  Joining does not give you edit rights. Members read and contribute; an Owner or Admin can raise
  your role afterwards. See [People and roles](/guide/organizations/people-and-roles).
</Callout>

## From the mobile app

The Android app covers the requester's side of all of this. You can browse the
directory, ask to join, and withdraw a request. Deciding requests is web only, so
an Owner or Admin still answers yours from a browser.

![The organization directory in the Android app, with a Joined tag on the organization the user belongs to](/img/mobile/organizations-directory.png)

Two ways in:

- **Profile → Organizations** opens the directory from anywhere.
- On **Home**, a card appears while you belong to no organization at all. It reads
  "Join your organization" and opens the same directory. Once you are a member it
  disappears on its own, and there is nothing to dismiss.

The directory lists your own organizations first, then everything else public. Type
in the search field and the list narrows after a moment; the search runs on the
server, so it matches descriptions, locations and types as well as names. Clearing
the field brings the whole directory back. Each row shows the organization's type,
location and member count, with a **Joined** or **Requested** tag when one applies
and **Private** on the organizations you belong to that are not listed publicly.

### Ask to join

Open an organization to see its profile, how many members it has, and how many of
its resources you can reach. **Request to join** opens a sheet with an optional
message of up to 250 characters, the same message an Owner or Admin reads beside
your name. Send it and the button is replaced by a pending banner.

![The Request to join sheet with an optional message and its character counter](/img/mobile/organizations-join-sheet.png)

You will not see a Request to join button on an organization you already belong to
or have already asked about, and neither will you on a private one. There is nothing
to press while you are offline either: the button is disabled with a note saying so,
because the request has to reach the server to mean anything.

### While your request is pending

The Home card changes to **Request pending** with the organization's name and opens
that organization when tapped. On the organization itself, **Cancel request** asks
you to confirm and then withdraws it. You are back to Request to join and can ask
again later.

You get an email when the request is decided, approved or rejected. There is no
in-app notification, so the app catches up the next time you open the
organization: approved and the Home card is gone, with your role shown on the
organization instead.

### Open on the web

Members, teams, settings and join requests are web features. The organization screen
has an **Open on web platform** button in its header that takes you to the same
organization in a browser.

## If you were invited instead

An invitation names an email address and a role, and puts you in without a request — once
you accept it. Invitations are answered on **Profile menu → Account → Invitations**,
which lists every one waiting for you with the role it offers; the link in the
invitation email takes you straight there. Select **Accept** on the row.

Nothing accepts it for you. Signing in does not, and neither does signing up from the
invitation email: create your account, and the invitation is still waiting on that tab.
That is deliberate — a membership gives you access to everything the organization owns,
and an Admin or Owner role makes you answerable for other people's work, so joining is
always something you choose.

The tab lists only what was sent to the address you sign in with. So if you signed up
with a different address than the one invited, the invitation will not find you and the
tab will look empty — ask for it to be re-sent to the address you actually sign in with.
